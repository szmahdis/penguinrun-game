#Design Pillars

##Setting

The player is chased by enemies in a mazelike city and has to collect tokens to
complete the level without getting caught.

The game is set in a bright and colourful environment. With colours assigned to each part of the map orientation of the player can be improved.


 ##Gameplay

Movement should be simple: Horizontal movement in all direction with the ability to jump

The camera should be in third person perspective so that the player can be more aware of their surroundings during gameplay
